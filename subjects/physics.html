Core Physics Modules

1. Mechanics & Motion

Kinematics graphs, projectile vectors, Newton's laws of motion, momentum conservation, and rotational dynamics.

2. Waves & Electricity

Superposition, diffraction gratings, Doppler effect, Kirchhoff's circuit laws, potential dividers, and AC circuits.

3. Thermal & Quantum

Ideal gas laws, first law of thermodynamics, photoelectric effect, de Broglie wavelength, and nuclear fission/fusion.

Paper-by-Paper Breakdown

O-Level (5054) & IGCSE (0625)

  • Paper 1 (MCQ): 40 multiple choice questions with elimination speed tactics.
  • Paper 2 (Theory): Structured explanation questions with explicit mark scheme keywords.
  • Paper 4 (Alternative to Practical): Experimental controls, scale graph plotting, and source of error evaluations.

A-Level (9702) Track

  • Paper 1 & 2 (AS Level): Core mechanics, materials, waves, and electricity.
  • Paper 4 (A2 Structured): Circular motion, gravitational fields, capacitance, quantum, and medical physics.
  • Paper 5 (Planning & Evaluation): Design experiments, identify independent/dependent variables, and analyze logarithmic error graphs.
