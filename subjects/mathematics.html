What Students Learn

1. Pure Algebraic Techniques

Quadratic factorizations, completing the square, polynomial division, binomial expansions, and complex algebraic fractions.

2. Coordinate & Trigonometry

Trigonometric identities, radian measure, circle theorems, vector equations of lines, and 3D geometric projections.

3. Differential & Integral Calculus

First principles differentiation, product and quotient rules, chain rule, definite integrals, and area under curves.

Course Structure by Paper

O-Level & IGCSE Track

  • Paper 1 (Non-Calculator): Mental arithmetic, estimation, algebraic fractions, indices, and transformation geometry.
  • Paper 2 (Calculator): Trigonometry, statistics histograms, probability tree diagrams, vectors, and kinematics matrices.

Cambridge A-Level Track (9709)

  • Paper 1 (Pure Maths 1): Quadratics, functions, coordinate geometry, circular measure, trigonometry, and calculus.
  • Paper 3 (Pure Maths 3): Algebra, logarithmic functions, numerical solutions, complex numbers, and differential equations.
  • Paper 4 (Mechanics) or Paper 5 (Probability & Stats): Forces, Newton's laws, energy, or normal distributions and permutations.
