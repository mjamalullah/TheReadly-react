Core Chemistry Modules

1. Physical Chemistry

Moles, stoichiometry, enthalpy changes (Hess's Law), reaction kinetics (rate equations), and chemical equilibria (Kc, Kp).

2. Inorganic & Periodicity

Trends in Period 3 oxides, Group 2 and Group 17 chemistry, transition element complexes, and ligand displacement.

3. Organic Reaction Mechanisms

Electrophilic addition, nucleophilic substitution (SN1/SN2), condensation polymerization, and NMR/IR spectroscopic analysis.
